# Supplementary datasets for uncalcined coal gangue–slag pastes

Material price observations and literature-based carbon emission factors associated with the manuscript:

**Bayesian Kolmogorov–Arnold networks link macroscopic–microscopic responses with carbon and cost efficiency in uncalcined coal gangue–slag pastes**

## Files

- [Material Prices Datasets.xlsx](Material%20Prices%20Datasets.xlsx): dated price observations for slag, sodium hydroxide, cement and sodium silicate, organized in four worksheets. Prices are recorded in Chinese yuan per tonne (CNY/t), with source URLs retained in each worksheet.
- [Carbon Emission Datasets.xlsx](Carbon%20Emission%20Datasets.xlsx): literature emission-factor records, including material labels, reported values and units, inventory-converted values and units, and literature sources.

Units and source-specific notes are provided in the workbooks and should be read together with the corresponding values.
